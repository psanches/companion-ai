# Lumi — Born in Brazil. Designed for the World.

Born in Brazil. Designed for the World.

Lumi is a Brazil-born artificial intelligence initiative with a global mission to develop an accessible, trustworthy, and compassionate digital companion for people living with Parkinson's disease.

Thoughtful conversations. Everyday organization. Information with sources. Built around people, one careful step at a time.

## Our story

Lumi was founded by Pedro Sanches, a technology and cloud professional with more than 20 years of international experience across Latin America, North America and Europe.

Personal proximity to the everyday realities of Parkinson’s inspired a practical question: how can technology offer a more approachable way to talk, organize the day and find understandable information?

Lumi brings that motivation together with an ambition to listen to people living with Parkinson’s, families and care partners. Professional experience informs the project; scientific and clinical expertise must guide its evaluation.

## What Lumi does

These capabilities are implemented in the development application or being evaluated there. They are not a claim of production readiness, clinical validation or proven health benefit.

### AI Companion

**Development functionality**

Natural conversations, supportive interactions, personalization and conversational continuity through saved history and memory.

Memory and account behavior still require ongoing privacy and usability review.

### Google Calendar

**Development functionality**

Calendar integration, appointment scheduling with confirmation, conflict checks and a current-week agenda support everyday organization.

Requires sign-in, Google authorization and a connected calendar. Real-account flows need continuing acceptance testing.

### Mind & Body

**Development functionality**

Physical and cognitive well-being activities offer ways to explore movement, attention and memory.

Demonstrative educational activities; not prescribed rehabilitation or clinically evaluated treatment.

### Health News

**Development functionality**

Five selected health stories are presented in Portuguese with identifiable sources and verification filters.

Selection and filtering continue to be refined. Sources can change and automated verification has limitations.

### Lumi Research

**Development functionality**

Access to current information and research with identifiable sources and transparent references.

Source quality, freshness and AI accuracy require human judgment; this is not a clinical literature review service.

### Accessibility

**Evaluation in progress**

A responsive desktop and Android interface, readable controls and continuing improvements to navigation and input.

Automated mobile viewport tests complement, but do not replace, real-device, assistive-technology and user evaluation.

### Privacy & Safety

**Evaluation in progress**

Medical safeguards and restricted medical-record routes are implemented in development, alongside responsible AI design.

Sensitive medical-record features remain blocked for real data. No completed privacy or clinical certification is claimed.

## Who it is for

### People living with Parkinson’s

A companion being developed around daily life, individuality and practical needs.

### Older adults

A calmer, more approachable way to interact with digital tools.

### Families & care partners

A shared interest in understandable information and everyday organization; no caregiver monitoring or delegated account access is currently promised.

## Our vision

Parkinson’s disease is our initial focus. Lumi is being designed with the ambition to support people around the world, with attention to language, cultural context and accessibility.

### Now — Develop and refine

Evaluate the companion, calendar, educational activities, news and research in the development environment.

### Next — Listen and evaluate

Seek appropriate scientific and clinical guidance, co-design with users and ethical usability and accessibility pilot opportunities.

### Long term — Explore responsibly

Consider other neurological conditions, including Alzheimer’s disease, other forms of dementia, multiple sclerosis and other neurodegenerative disorders.

Expansion is a future ambition, dependent on appropriate scientific, clinical and user-centered evaluation. No efficacy, regulatory approval or readiness for these conditions is claimed.

## Responsible AI

### A companion, not a clinician

Lumi is AI. It does not diagnose, prescribe, replace professional care or provide emergency services. Generated answers may be incomplete or wrong.

### Privacy by design, under evaluation

This institutional preview has no account system, analytics or data-collection form. The separate application has its own authentication and safeguards. Do not enter real medical records in experimental features.

### Accessibility is ongoing work

Large controls, readable typography, keyboard access and responsive layouts are design commitments. Evaluation with real users and assistive technologies remains necessary.

### Evidence before claims

No clinical validation or proven health benefit is asserted. Educational information and automatically translated content need appropriate human review.
